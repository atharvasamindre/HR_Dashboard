## 🧭 Introduction

This repository hosts a collection of Tableau dashboards, each designed to address specific business and analytical needs. From **Sales** and **Customer Insights** to **HR Analytics**, these projects demonstrate the power of data storytelling through interactive and insightful visualizations.

Dive into dashboards that explore datasets from various domains and uncover trends, patterns, and strategic insights.

---

## 📁 Project Structure

Each Tableau project in the repository follows a consistent structure:

- `[Project Name]/`
  - `project_name.twb`: Tableau workbook containing the dashboard
  - `data.csv` or `data.xlsx`: Dataset used for analysis
  - `presentation.pptx`: PowerPoint summarizing insights and outcomes
  - `README.md`: Project-specific documentation, including objectives, approach, and key findings

## 📚 Projects Included

### 📈 Sales Dashboard

#### 🎯 Purpose
Analyze year-over-year sales performance and understand sales trends.

#### ✅ Features
- Total sales, profits, quantity (current vs previous year)
- Monthly trends for each KPI
- Highlight best/worst-performing months
- Product subcategory comparison with profit
- Weekly analysis with average comparison
- Dynamic year filter and product/location filters

---

### 👥 Customer Dashboard

#### 🎯 Purpose
Understand customer behavior, loyalty, and satisfaction.

#### ✅ Features
- KPIs: Total customers, sales per customer, number of orders
- Monthly customer trends
- Distribution based on number of orders
- Top 10 customers by profit (with ranks, sales, orders, last purchase)
- Filters by year, region, and category

---

### 🧑‍💼 HR Dashboard

#### 🎯 User Story
As an HR manager, I want a comprehensive dashboard to analyze workforce trends and employee data.

#### ✅ Summary View

**Overview**
- Total hired, active, terminated employees
- Trends over years
- Breakdown by department and job title
- HQ vs branches (New York as HQ)
- Location distribution (city/state)

**Demographics**
- Gender ratio
- Age groups and education levels
- Education vs performance correlation

**Income Analysis**
- Salary comparisons by gender/education
- Age vs salary by department

#### 📋 Employee Records View
- Table view with filters by name, department, age, salary, etc.
